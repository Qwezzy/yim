About

Yakha Ikusasa Manje

An NPO registered as a Section 21 company since August 2004. Primary health care and HIV/AIDS services, then social development and education.

Why we exist

YIM started because people in Kingsway, Lindelani and Barcelona informal settlements had no local organisation for HIV care and family support.

Registered standing

  • NPC / Section 21 company
  • NPO with the Department of Social Development
  • Public Benefit Organisation with SARS
