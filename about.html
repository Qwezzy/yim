About

Yakha Ikusasa Manje

A registered South African NPO working through education, health, psychosocial support and livelihood programmes.

Why we exist

YIM was established in 2004 as a Section 21 company because families in Kingsway, Lindelani and Barcelona informal settlements had no local organisation for HIV care and family support.

The work now reaches Daveyton, Ekurhuleni and Benoni; Mkhondo and Amsterdam; Rorke’s Drift and KwaThandeka.

Director Hlobi Ngidi leads the organisation. Board: Nolupho Mgudlwa (chair), Mcebo Khaliswayo, Nomonde Hele, Sfiso Mdluli and Phumzile Kalala.
