Education outcomes

More than 4,500 learners were reached through 26 schools and ECD centres. Mentorship focused on leadership, time management, independent thinking, communication and personal growth. Several learners progressed to tertiary study or work.

Gauteng hosted a self-awareness workshop at Kingsway Secondary, ran Winter School with snacks to keep attendance up, and celebrated three Grade 12 beneficiaries who received top academic awards. Four KZN participants enrolled in teaching courses because of the programme.

The next education goals named in the reports are computer literacy and robotics, more branches in underserved areas, stronger partner collaboration, and scholarships for learners who cannot pay fees.

Health and psychosocial support

HTS

Over 22,621 HIV tests by Q2 2024–25. Thousands of male and female condoms distributed. Improved linkage-to-care and closer work with WITS RHI. 2023 also recorded 234,000 male and 6,100 female condoms in a single reporting cycle, and a full World AIDS Day programme on 1 December.

Psychosocial

Dialogues, awareness campaigns, household support, referrals into training or work, and support groups. Stigma around disclosure, reluctance to start ART, supply shortages and unemployment remain the main constraints named in the report.

Food and livelihoods

Gardens were established across Gauteng, Mpumalanga and KwaZulu-Natal. Produce was sold to vendors and local shops. A solar borehole went in in KZN. Forty-five beneficiaries — mostly women and youth — received agricultural training and livelihood support.

Mpumalanga’s SEF work added recycling, home-tracing for clinic medication, and school cleaning. Lessons recorded: community ownership keeps gardens alive; infrastructure planning matters as much as seed.

What YIM is building next

Programmes

Expand after-school and literacy work, add digital learning, strengthen psychosocial support, grow HIV prevention and food security, and open more youth opportunities.

Systems

Stronger monitoring, a team pact across provinces, staff development, and diversified funding so programmes do not stall when a single contract is late.

Sustainability

Strategic partnerships, community ownership, skills transfer, income from gardens, and organisational capacity so the work outlasts any one grant.
