Three pillars

The new mark is five people holding a common centre. The site uses those five colours: green, gold, blue, red and black.

Health

HIV testing and linkage to care, STI support, HTS, condom distribution, sexual and reproductive health, and PMTCT.

Social

Food parcels, psychosocial support, home visits, community dialogues, and care for orphans and vulnerable children.

Education

After-school tutoring, life skills and cultural programmes for learners who need a safe place to work after class.

A healthy, socially developed and educated South Africa.

YIM started because people in Kingsway, Lindelani and Barcelona had no local organisation for HIV care and family support.

  • Home-based care
  • Support groups that reduce isolation and stigma
  • Food gardens and parcels
  • After-school space for homework, sport and arts
