Four streams of work

From the 2024–2025 annual report: education, health, psychosocial support and food security.

Education

After-school support, literacy and numeracy, 30 reading clubs, ECD support and 82 teaching assistants across 26 sites. YIM Education began in 2017.

Health

HIV testing, TB and STI screening, ART linkage, PrEP referrals and condom distribution — with WITS RHI and the Department of Health.

Social & food

Home visits, support groups, parcels, and IDC gardens that trained women and youth and installed a solar borehole in KwaZulu-Natal.

A healthy, socially developed and educated South Africa.

Started in 2004 for families in Kingsway, Lindelani and Barcelona. The same model now runs in Ekurhuleni, Mkhondo, Amsterdam, Rorke’s Drift and KwaThandeka.
