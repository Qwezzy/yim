Events

Days the community gathers

Dates below follow the 2023 and 2024–25 calendars. Confirm times with info@yim.org.za before travelling.

YIM treats public days as programme work: testing tents go up on World AIDS Day, learners rehearse for Heritage Celebration, and Winter School keeps the after-school rhythm through the holidays.

Lights On After School is the national campaign YIM joined on 26 October to argue that after-school sites should stay funded. ECD graduation days are hosted with partner centres.

30 September

Heritage Celebration

First hosted at Kingsway in 2023 — music, drama, poetry, dance and public speaking by after-school beneficiaries.

1 December

World AIDS Day

Testing, education and community participation. A fixed point in the HTS calendar, with Department of Health partners on site.

School holidays

Winter School

Two-week academic and non-academic programme. Snacks are part of the design so attendance holds through the holiday.

26 October

Lights On After School

YIM has joined the national campaign that argues for keeping after-school programmes running when the school bell goes.

16 December

Day of Reconciliation

A public gathering in Gauteng. Ask the office for the year’s venue.

Last week of July

Groom a boy to be a man

A social-development gathering named in the 2023 future-plans section, alongside community clothing exchanges.
