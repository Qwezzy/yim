Programmes

Health, school, home and garden.

Figures from the 2024–2025 annual report, with 2023 results where they still describe the same work.

After school

YIM Education started in 2017. In 2024–25 more than 4,500 learners were supported across 26 schools and ECD centres, with 82 teaching assistants and 30 reading clubs. KZN has reached 4,240 learners across 11 schools in recent years.

Health

By Q2 of 2024–25 more than 22,621 HIV tests had been done. In one 2023 quarter the team ran 5,997 tests and issued 234,000 male and 6,100 female condoms. Partners include the Department of Health and WITS RHI.

Social development

Home visits, childcare forums, counselling and parcels. In 2023 the social team served about 100 households a month.

Food security

IDC gardening trained women and youth across three provinces. 45 beneficiaries. Produce sold locally. A solar-powered borehole was installed in KwaZulu-Natal.
